Tamil Saiva devotional song (bhakti), Carnatic style in raga Mohanam
(major pentatonic: S R2 G3 P D2), joyful temple chariot-festival mood.

Lead male vocal with warm, devotional delivery; small chorus answering
the refrain "Chithirath ther erinaai Sivane". Nadaswaram (South Indian
double-reed) plays interludes and shadows the vocal line. Thavil
provides a driving 8-beat (adi tala) festival rhythm, with tavil rolls
at phrase endings. Tanpura drone on the tonic throughout. Optional
small cymbals (talam) keeping the beat.

Medium-fast tempo (~96 bpm), celebratory and majestic, like a temple
procession. Builds in energy verse by verse, ends on a grand sustained
nadaswaram phrase over the drone.
